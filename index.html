    How to play

    You get seven prompts a day across Marvel, DC, Invincible and The Boys. Comics, shows, movies, games and cartoons all count.

    You have 25 seconds per prompt. Guess as many times as you like. Wrong guesses cost nothing but time, and your first accepted answer locks in. Any correct answer scores, but the obvious ones are worth almost nothing, and so is the famous "obscure" pick every fan reaches for. That one lands in Canon for 15.

    Your hero waits in orbit above the city. Lock in a correct answer and the hero dives down through comic history. Every panel deeper is an older era of printing, and the deeper the fall, the rarer your answer. Miss, and your hero stays up in orbit. Fall all the way to the bare black and white city at the bottom and you've found the 1 in Kryptillion answer. It stays secret, so nobody can pass it along.

    Close misspellings get autocorrected in the box. Press Enter again to lock in the corrected answer. Real names and last names work too. If an answer is too vague, like a title several characters share, you'll be asked to be more specific.

    Fan-made and unofficial. Not affiliated with Marvel, DC, Image Comics, Skybound, Dynamite, Amazon or Krillion, which inspired the format.
